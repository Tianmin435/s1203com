Engine models reuse the Saturn V F-1/J-2 assets, Falcon 9 Merlin geometry, and SLS RS-25/RL10 geometry. Falcon 9 fairing and landing-leg models reuse the source explorer's procedural geometry; the Apollo Launch Abort System reuses its Saturn V model node. Crew spacecraft reuse Apollo nodes, the supplied Orion GLB, and the Shuttle Orbiter groups. Satellite uses assets/space_satellite.glb. Shuttle models use assets/space-shuttle-challenger/source/Challenger.glb and Orion uses assets/orion_spacecraft.glb.
