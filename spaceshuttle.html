External tank · front to aft

LOX · intertank · LH2

The forward tank stores liquid oxygen; the intertank connects the propellant tanks and booster attachments; the large aft tank stores liquid hydrogen. Together they hold about 526,126 US gallons and are jettisoned after powered flight.

Solid rocket boosters · pair

Four motor segments each

Each booster has a forward skirt with recovery parachutes, a four-segment solid motor, and an aft skirt with a steerable nozzle. They burn for about two minutes, separate, descend by parachute, and are recovered for refurbishment.

Orbiter · forward to aft

Crew · payload · propulsion

The pressurized crew module is forward and normally carries up to seven crew; the payload bay occupies the mid-fuselage, and the aft section carries three main engines, two OMS pods, and the body flap. A 50-ft Remote Manipulator System handled payloads in orbit.
