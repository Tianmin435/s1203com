Dataset integrity note

The supplied catalogue explicitly says its modular tanks and recoloured liquid/hybrid boosters are game-style, display-only models. They are retained as dataset rows because the request covers every catalogue component, but their “real hardware” fields say not defined instead of inventing dimensions, mass, or performance.

Some development engines (F‑1A, J‑2S, J‑2X, Merlin 1B, RL10C-3) did not fly in the exact form shown. Status labels distinguish flown, cancelled/development, and current production/operational families.
